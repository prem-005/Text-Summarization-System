In today’s digital world, an enormous amount of information is generated every day through newspapers, research papers, websites, blogs, social media, online articles, and various digital platforms. Text summarization is a Natural Language Processing (NLP) technique that automatically converts a long piece of text into a shorter and meaningful summary while preserving the important information and overall context of the original content. The proposed system takes a large input document or paragraph from the user and processes the text using various NLP preprocessing techniques such as sentence segmentation, tokenization, stop-word removal, punctuation handling, and text normalization. Therefore, an automated text summarization system reduces reading time, improves information accessibility, and helps users focus on the most important information contained within large volumes of text.
